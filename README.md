# Folio

Folio is an AI librarian for ensembles, on iPads. The piece of music is the identity: import a scan or a digital
score and the librarian reads it, numbers every bar, and cuts a part for every stand. Marks are made at the level
of the bar and appear on every stand live, and a conductor's spoken rehearsal note becomes a proposed mark on the
right stands only. Built solo by CK Jiang for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, October 27 to November 4,
2026; the eight commits dated October 26 are setup only.

Meta models: Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0.

## Try it

App: https://folio-app.jckjjcckk.com (best in Safari on an iPad; the native build is on TestFlight). Shared judge
account: `judges@folio.jckjjcckk.com` / `testpassword`. Its shelf holds a string quartet in practice mode and a Bach
minuet converted from a scan. One librarian at a time. The account has a small Meta spend cap; if it runs
out, email folio@jckjjcckk.com.
